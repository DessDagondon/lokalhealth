How to use these results

Monthly forecasts and barangay seasonal patterns are the main planning view. Weekly alerts are a separate, faster-changing reference.

Monthly forecast

The next-month estimate carries the latest reported month's confirmed count forward. The same-month historical median is shown as a comparison, not used to calculate the forecast.

Barangay clusters

Barangays are grouped by the distribution of their confirmed records across morbidity months. Groups describe recorded timing patterns, not severity, risk, or future case counts.

Complementary weekly alert

The one-week naïve estimate carries the latest recorded week's count forward. It does not learn a trend or estimate the probability of an epidemic, and its result is not 100% certain. Treat weekly forecasts and risk tiers as awareness signals for possible dengue activity, not a guarantee, diagnosis, or official outbreak declaration.

Data limitations

The database does not track zero-case submissions or reporting completeness. Months and weeks without records are gaps, not verified zeroes. Verify recent reports and follow local health-officer guidance before making decisions.
