How to analyse these results

What the dashboard doesIt summarizes the records entered, compares citywide weekly confirmed cases with historical P50/P75 thresholds, and helps prioritize barangays for review. Thresholds show how this week compares with the available historical baseline; they do not confirm an outbreak.

What the naïve forecast meansIt carries the latest recorded week's confirmed count forward as next week's estimate. It does not learn a trend or estimate the probability of an epidemic, and its result is not 100% certain.

How to act on itTreat the forecast and risk tier as awareness signals for possible dengue activity as not a guarantee, diagnosis, or official outbreak declaration. Verify recent reports and follow local health-officer guidance before making decisions.

Data limitationsOnly confirmed cases set risk tiers; suspected cases are shown for context. The database does not track zero-case submissions. Missing current-year weeks are gaps, not confirmed zeroes; missing historical weeks are treated as zero under the thesis method.
